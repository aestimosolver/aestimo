# Authors and contributors

The project was initiated by Sefer Bora Lisesivdin. Major contributions include
Robert Steed's solver and tutorial work, Hamza Hebal's solver and GUI work,
and contributions from Beyza Lisesivdin and Zbigniew Koziol.

The [GitHub contribution history](https://github.com/aestimosolver/aestimo/graphs/contributors)
records subsequent work. The release-specific names below are retained from
the original website. Affiliations and contact details on that historical site
may describe earlier circumstances; they are not presented as a current directory.
